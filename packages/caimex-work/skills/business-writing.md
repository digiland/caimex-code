# Business writing

- **Lead with the point.** First sentence says what you want or what happened.
- **One idea per paragraph**, short sentences, plain words. No filler ("I hope this finds you well").
- **Make the ask explicit**: who does what by when.
- **British English** spelling (organisation, colour), 24-hour times with a time zone when it matters, dates as 24 September 2026.
- **Numbers**: currency code or symbol and period on every figure (USD 1.2m, Q3 2026).
- **Tone**: warm and direct, never casual with external people.

Formats:

- **Email** — subject line that states the topic and ask; 3–8 sentences; sign-off.
- **Memo** — To / From / Date / Subject, then Purpose, Background, Recommendation, Next steps.
- **Proposal** — Problem, Proposed approach, Scope and timeline, Cost, Risks, Decision needed.

Save drafts to `drafts/YYYY-MM-DD-<short-name>.md`. Never send anything; the user sends it.
